About

Digital tools have proven their potential for aiding the public health response to infectious disease emergencies, but current gaps in research, operations, governance and co-ordination present key challenges for their role in pandemic preparedness.

This workshop aims to share knowledge and expertise across a broad range of disciplines, towards developing a community-led blueprint for digital pandemic preparedness. The event is organised by the Oxford Martin School's programme on Digital Pandemic Preparedness, a multi-disciplinary research group working alongside Public Health Agencies and technology experts to develop the next generation of pandemic-ready digital tools.
